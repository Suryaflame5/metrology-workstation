## 1. Operational Structure & Merchant of Record (MoR) Strategy

For an independent software developer operating from India selling globally:
- **Merchant of Record (Dodo Payments / Lemon Squeezy / Paddle)** acts as the legal entity selling to the end consumer.
- **Global Tax Compliance**: The MoR calculates, collects, and remits indirect taxes (EU VAT, US State Sales Tax, UK VAT, Australian GST) globally.
- **Cross-Border Payouts**: The MoR converts international customer funds into INR/USD payouts sent directly to the developer's registered Indian bank account.

---

## 2. Indian Regulatory & Banking Baseline

1. **Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA)**:
   - Digital software exports require proper remittance tracking (FIRC / e-BRC / Payment Gateway Settlement Advice).
   - Inward remittances must match legitimate export of digital services / software licenses.
2. **GST (Goods & Services Tax)**:
   - Export of services outside India is treated as "Zero Rated Supply" under GST.
   - Formal GSTIN registration is mandatory when aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 Lakhs (or immediately if seeking Letter of Undertaking [LUT] for zero-rated export without paying IGST upfront).
3. **Income Tax**:
   - Commercial net income is declared under Section 44ADA (Presumptive Taxation for specified professionals) or standard business profit & loss accounting.

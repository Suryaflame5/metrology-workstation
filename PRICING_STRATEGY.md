## 1. Value Proposition & Market Context

Traditional metrology calibration software packages (e.g. Fluke MET/CAL, IndySoft, PQ Systems GAGEpack) typically cost between **$3,500 to $12,000+ per seat** upfront, with mandatory annual maintenance fees of 20% ($700–$2,400/yr), plus weeks of expensive professional onboarding.

**Metrology Workstation** provides:
- **Instant Deployment**: Modern Windows 10/11 MSIX desktop application with zero database server setup.
- **Audit-Grade Mathematical Rigor**: 50-digit exact decimal arithmetic, full JCGM 100/101/106 concordance, and ANSI/NCSL Z540.3 Method 6 decision guardbanding.
- **Zero Cloud Lock-in**: 100% local-first and air-gapped capable.

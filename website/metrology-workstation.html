Precision Metrology Desktop Workstation

An evidence-first calculation and measurement uncertainty platform engineered from first principles for calibration laboratories, standards organizations, and high-reliability aerospace/defense quality teams.

01 · MATHEMATICAL RIGOR

50-Digit Exact Decimal Context

Standard IEEE 754 floating-point arithmetic introduces catastrophic precision loss and representation errors in recursive metrological calculations. Metrology Workstation enforces an exact 50-digit decimal arithmetic context across all Type A/B standard uncertainty evaluations and covariance matrix transformations.

02 · STANDARDS CONCORDANCE

JCGM 100/106 & ANSI Z540.3

Complete concordance with international standards: GUM uncertainty propagation (JCGM 100:2008 §5), Welch-Satterthwaite effective degrees of freedom (Annex G), and ANSI/NCSL Z540.3-2006 Method 5 and Method 6 guardbanding curves enforcing consumer risk ($P_{\text{CR}} \le 2.0\%$).

03 · AUDITABILITY

12-Stage Mathematical Replay

Every saved calibration record generates an immutable 12-stage derivation trace. Auditors can independently replay, step-by-step, every raw measurement, standard deviation, sensitivity coefficient, degrees of freedom, coverage factor, and guardband boundary.

04 · LOCAL-FIRST SECURITY

Zero-Cloud Air-Gapped Operation

Engineered for secure, isolated metrology laboratories. All SQLite databases, audit ledgers, and evidence archives reside strictly in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MetrologyWorkstation\. Operates 100% offline with zero cloud telemetry or external network dependencies.

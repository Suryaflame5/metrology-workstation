Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Sovereign Local-First Architecture

Metrology Workstation is engineered from the ground up as a 100% local desktop application. All calibration databases, measurement readings, uncertainty models, laboratory procedures, evidence packages, and audit trails remain exclusively on your local storage in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MetrologyWorkstation\.

2. Zero Data Collection & Telemetry

The software does not collect, transmit, or monitor telemetry, analytical telemetry, usage metrics, or calculation parameters. The desktop binary functions in fully air-gapped environments with zero external network connectivity.

3. Purchase & Payment Data

When purchasing commercial licenses, payment processing and invoicing are conducted exclusively through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant Merchant of Record partners (e.g. Dodo Payments / Lemon Squeezy). NovyraX never receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials.
